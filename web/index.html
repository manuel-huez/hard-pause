How it works

Make the choice.
Keep the pause.

Choose your limits before a craving hits. Once your plan starts, you can’t remove its blocks or shorten the wait. Changing your mind takes time.

  1. 01

    Pick what keeps pulling you back.

    Social apps, video sites, adult websites. Choose what to block and give your plan a name.

  2. 02

    Decide how long you’ll wait.

    Set the wait for a break or for ending your plan. Once you start, you can’t shorten it.

  3. 03

    Close the window. Do your thing.

    Your plan keeps running. If you ask for access, the wait begins. Your apps stay blocked until it ends.

Good to know

Before you
start.

You choose the limits.
Hard Pause helps you keep them.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Ask to end your plan and the wait you chose will begin. It can’t be shortened. If you chose Pause, you can request a short break too.

What if I have more than one plan?

You can make separate plans for work, evenings, or anything else. If two plans block the same app or site, both must allow access before it opens again.

Can it block adult content?

Yes. Turn on adult website blocking in your plan. On Mac, Hard Pause checks sites against an updated adult website list on your device. You can add specific sites too. No filter catches every adult site.

Does Hard Pause send my activity anywhere?

No. Blocking and website checks run entirely on your device, using its built-in protections. Hard Pause does not send your browsing activity to us or anyone else. No account. No tracking.

How strong is the blocking?

Hard Pause is still in development. Some browser setups can get around website blocks, and someone with Mac administrator access can turn off the protection. Save your work before blocking apps: closing them can lose unsaved changes.

Can I use it on my iPhone?

The iPhone and iPad app is in development for iOS 26 or later. It is not available in the App Store yet, and its protection still needs testing on a real iPhone.
