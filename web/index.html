NATIVE ON BOTH PLATFORMS

One rule:
access takes time.

Hard Pause uses local system protection designed for each device.

01iOS 26 or later

iPhone and iPad

Screen Time keeps selected apps and websites blocked, even while Hard Pause is closed.

  • Selected apps and website domains
  • Independent named blocks and delays
  • Optional device-wide app-removal protection

Screen Time permission remains under iOS control.

02macOS 26 or later

Mac

A protected background service keeps your rules active when the app closes. No browser extension is required.

  • Selected apps and exact website domains
  • Independent named blocks and delays
  • Protection continues after the app closes

An exact domain does not include every subdomain. A Mac administrator can remove the service. Closing a selected app can interrupt unsaved work.

Quiet by design.
Local by default.

PRIVATE

Your rules stay on your device.

No account, analytics, browsing history, cloud service, or remote classification.

HONEST

Protection limits stay visible.

Hard Pause reports permission, storage, and enforcement failures in the app.

INDEPENDENT

One block cannot release another.

Overlapping rules remain until every block that contains them allows access.
